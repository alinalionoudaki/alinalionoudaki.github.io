Publications & Research Abstract

York, May 2010

“On the derivative of the EGG Signal for determining GCIs and GOIs”

MSc Thesis Abstract

Voice quality is a complex attribute of voice arising from the regularity and duration of the closed phase from vocal fold cycle to cycle. Accurate determination of glottal closure (GCI) and glottal opening (GOI) instants is crucial. This work introduces a robust algorithm for glottal instant detection from Electroglottographic (EGG) signals and investigates interactions between speech AM-FM components and glottal phases.

By utilizing "Slope Filtering" based on spectral differentiation rather than standard derivatives, the algorithm reliably highlights major slope-filtered peaks even in noisy recordings. Furthermore, an adaptive Quasi-Harmonic Model (QHM) representation extracts instantaneous AM-FM components correlated with EGG glottal phases, proving high accuracy across diverse speech segments and speakers.
